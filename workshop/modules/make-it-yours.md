# Module: Make it yours (re-theming the chief of staff)

The proof that the architecture is right: you can swap the *entire* persona and
company by editing **data files only** — no skill changes. We did exactly this,
turning a generic founder pack into **Pied Piper**.

## The four files that make it "you"

1. **`workspace/USER.md`** — who the principal is (Richard: founder/CEO, mission,
   what annoys him, key relationships).
2. **`workspace/IDENTITY.md`** — who the assistant is (Anton: calm, decisive on
   ops, deferential on strategy; "Son of Anton" Easter egg).
3. **`clawchief/priority-map.md`** — the big one. Your real people + programs +
   urgency + action mode. ~80% of "making it yours" is here.
4. **`clawchief/tasks.md`** — seed it with your real current work.

Programs became: Middle-Out compression, first paying users, PiperNet,
fundraising, the Hooli lawsuit, dev-adoption partnerships, launches/PR.

## How the agent uses it (verified)

Asked for "today's top 3 priorities," Anton read `priority-map.md` + `tasks.md`
and returned Richard/Anton-owned items pulled straight from the data — the skills
were untouched. That's the whole lesson: **policy + state are data; workflow is
code; persona is data.**

## Exercise (the core workshop activity)

Each attendee replaces the Pied Piper people/programs in `priority-map.md` with
their **own**, reseeds `tasks.md`, edits `USER.md`/`IDENTITY.md`, restarts, and
asks their chief of staff for today's priorities. If the answer reflects their
real life, they've built it.

## Anti-patterns

- Don't hardcode names into `skills/` — skills stay generic and shareable.
- Don't invent priority labels that don't map to a real person/program.
- Don't let `tasks.md` drift — it's the single live source of truth.
